# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, constructive, and welcoming for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Share evidence and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Protect confidential and personal data.
- Help newer contributors learn without humiliation.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, or publishing private information without permission are not tolerated.

## Enforcement
Report serious conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the security contact listed in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
